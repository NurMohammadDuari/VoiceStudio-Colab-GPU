Cost, privacy, speed and commercial use.

FAQ

Does this cost anything?

No. Free Colab T4, free Cloudflare quick tunnel, and the app is AGPL-3.0 free. The paid VoiceStudio "Pro" plan sells a packaged version of what this repo sets up.

Is my audio private?

While a run is active, your audio is on the Colab VM and passes over the tunnel from Google to your PC — both HTTPS. Your PC never stores it locally. Models, settings and cloned voices sit in your Google Drive. Nothing is sent to the vendor.

Does it phone home?

The app has opt-in analytics that is off by default (box 3 explicitly records "no"). If you ever accept it, it sends engine usage, timings and error types — never audio or text.

Why is GPU usage so low?

Normal, and not a waste. See below.

Why is GPU usage so low?

The TTS model is ~2.4 GB, so ~4 GB of the 15 GB T4 is expected. VRAM usage is not a speed dial — unused VRAM does not make anything faster. Speed comes from kernels, which is what GPU Turbo (FlashInfer) improves: roughly 2× faster generations, enabled by default in box 1.

A T4 also idles between pipeline stages, because chunking, WAV post-processing and I/O are CPU work. Low utilisation between bursts is expected.

Why is Max slower than Balanced?

That is what Max is: 64 sampling steps plus postprocess, 8-beam ASR, 8-path dictation, NLLB translation with 8 beams. It is the slowest, highest-quality preset. Use GPU Turbo to make it bearable, or drop to Balanced for speed.

How long do I get?

Whatever Colab gives you — commonly up to ~12 GPU-hours per day, with idle tabs disconnected sooner. Box 2's heartbeat (every 2 minutes) reduces disconnects but cannot beat the hard cap. For 24/7, rent a GPU box; nothing about the setup changes except the URL.

Will my key keep working after Colab restarts?

Yes, if you enabled Drive persistence (default). The key is stored at /content/drive/omnivoice/api_key.txt and reloaded by box 2, so the one already on your PC stays valid. Only the URL changes.

Can I use this from more than one computer?

Yes. That is VoiceStudio Pro's "multiple devices" feature, and it costs nothing here. Install the app on the other machine, run the launcher, paste the same URL and key. (Pro's paid limit of 3 devices does not apply to the free app.)

Can I sell this?

Not with the default models. k2-fsa/OmniVoice is CC-BY-NC with commercial inference restricted, and the tokenizer has its own terms. Selling a dubbing or TTS service on that stack is not permitted.

To sell, you would need commercially licensed models, and note that if you modify the app and host it, AGPL-3.0 requires offering your modified source to users. VoiceStudio's own Pro and Cloud products exist for that; Cloud is still a waitlist.

Which models get downloaded?

k2-fsa/OmniVoice (TTS), eustlb/higgs-audio-v2-tokenizer, Systran/faster-whisper-large-v3 (ASR), two sherpa-onnx models (dictation), facebook/nllb-200-distilled-600M (translation) — about 5 GB. Optionally three pyannote repos for dubbing. Box 4 prints what is actually on disk.

Why must I accept a licence before generating?

Models whose licence is not commercial fail closed until you acknowledge the terms. That acknowledgement is yours to make: box 3 ships with ACCEPT_MODEL_LICENCES = False and will not record it on your behalf. It is a local honesty gate, not a security boundary — but it is enforced on use, so generation is blocked until it is recorded.

Do I need a Hugging Face account?

Only for dubbing (pyannote repos are gated). Everything else works without one.

Why do some buttons stay greyed out?

A small set of capabilities act on the backend host's filesystem and can never run for a remote client, by design. Saving audio to your PC is unaffected — that is an HTTP download. See HOW-IT-WORKS-IN-THE-APP.md.

Can I use a paid GPU instead of Colab?

Yes — anything that runs Linux with a GPU: a VPS, RunPod, Vast.ai, a spare machine. Run box 1 and 2 there (skip the Drive part if you do not need it), open a tunnel, paste the URL and key. The app does not care where the backend lives.

Is this the same as VoiceStudio Cloud?

No. Cloud is the vendor's hosted product and is still a waitlist. This is the open-source app with your own compute behind it.
